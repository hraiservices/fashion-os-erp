💡 Rules & Pro Tips

WhatsApp selling ke golden rules — in follow karo, results aayenge

✅ ALWAYS DO THIS

  • Personalized message bhejo — naam leke baat karo
  • Morning 10–12 ya evening 5–7 baje bhejo
  • WhatsApp *bold* use karo key points ke liye
  • Ek message mein ek hi call-to-action rakho
  • Sawaal poochho — response rate badhti hai
  • Demo ke baad 30 min mein follow-up karo
  • Har message ke baad note rakho CRM mein
  • Client ki language mein baat karo (Hindi/Urdu)

❌ NEVER DO THIS

  • Price pehle message mein mat batao
  • Same message copy-paste mat karo sab ko
  • Raat 9 baje ke baad message mat karo
  • 3 se zyada unanswered follow-up mat karo
  • Paragraph pe paragraph mat likhoo — short rakho
  • Desperate ya begging tone mat use karo
  • Jhoothe offers ya fake deadlines mat do
  • WhatsApp groups mein spam mat karo

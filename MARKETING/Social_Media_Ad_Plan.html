4-Phase Launch Strategy

Week-by-week campaign rollout

1

Awareness

Week 1–2
  • Set up FB & IG Business pages
  • Install Meta Pixel on website
  • Run awareness video ads
  • Post 3x/week organic content
  • Start Google Search ads
  • Budget: ₹5,000
2

Engagement

Week 3–4
  • Retarget video viewers (50%+ watch)
  • Run lead generation ads
  • Post Reels — before/after content
  • WhatsApp CTA on all ads
  • Collect first 50 demo leads
  • Budget: ₹8,000
3

Conversion

Week 5–8
  • Conversion campaigns (leads → demo)
  • Testimonial video ads
  • Google Display retargeting
  • Special offer ad — limited time
  • Target: 20 paying clients
  • Budget: ₹12,000
4

Scale

Month 3+
  • Scale winning ad sets 2x budget
  • Lookalike audiences (1–3%)
  • Referral campaign — word of mouth
  • YouTube Shorts ads
  • Target: 50+ active clients

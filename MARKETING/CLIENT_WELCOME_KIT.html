🚀 Quick Start — 3 Steps to Get Going
1

Open your app and create your account

Go to your URL above → Click "First time? Create account" → Enter your email and a password → Check your email for a confirmation link → Click it to verify → Sign in.

💡 You will automatically become the Admin — the first account always gets full access.
2

Set up your shop

After signing in → Go to Settings ⚙️ → Fill in your Shop Name, upload your logo, add your tailors' names, and set your rate card. Takes about 3 minutes.

3

Add your first order

Click the + New Order button → Enter customer name, mobile number, garment type, measurements, and delivery date → Save. Your order is live!

📲 Install on Your Phone (Recommended)

📲 Install as a Mobile App — It's Free!

No app store needed. Install directly from your browser and use it like a native app — with a home screen icon, offline access, and full-screen mode.

📱 Android (Chrome)

Open your URL in Chrome → Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) → Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen" → Tap Install.

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

Open your URL in Safari → Tap the Share button (□↑) → Scroll down → Tap "Add to Home Screen" → Tap Add.

💻 Desktop (Chrome)

Open your URL → Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar → Click it → Click Install.

💡 Tip

Once installed, open the app and tap Sign In with Mobile — your tailors can log in with just their phone number + password.

💡 Tips for Best Results
📌 Always add a delivery date when creating an order — it helps you see which orders are due soon.
📌 Use the Kanban board (Board view) to drag orders between stages visually.
📌 Save customer measurements in the CRM once — they auto-fill on the next order.
📌 The Activity Log shows a full history of every change made by any staff member.
📌 Use WhatsApp share on the order to send a delivery reminder directly to the customer.

Before You Spend One Rupee

Fix These — Today, Not After Launch

Every lead you generate from here on lands on these two sites. If they're broken or look unfinished, you burn the lead permanently — there's no second first impression. This costs you time, not money.

⚠️

stitch.himanshurajput.com — confirm load speed on real devices

You confirmed it works fine for real visitors, which is good — but double-check it on a cheap Android phone on 4G (not WiFi, not your dev machine), since that's the device most tailoring shop owners actually use. A 3+ second load or a flash of "LOADING…" text is exactly the kind of thing that makes a non-technical 45-year-old darzi owner close the tab and assume the app is broken.

  • Test on a real 4G connection, not office WiFi
  • If there's a visible "Loading..." flash, add a simple branded spinner/splash so it looks intentional, not broken
  • Test the full first-run flow: open app → see Kanban board → add a test order, on a phone you don't use daily
⚠️

himanshurajput.com — remove placeholder content before linking to it from any ad or WhatsApp message

This is your personal brand site and it's where "Explore STITCH Pro" sends visitors. Right now it's showing placeholder data that actively undermines trust: a fake email, a fake phone number, and "0+" for every stat (Apps Delivered, Happy Clients, Countries). A prospect who clicks through from a WhatsApp pitch and lands on "0+ Happy Clients" will assume you're not real.

  • Replace himanshu@example.com with your real email (swarooponline14@gmail.com or a himanshurajput.com address)
  • Replace the wa.me/919876543210 placeholder number with your actual WhatsApp Business number, everywhere it appears
  • Either populate the stats honestly (even "Pre-launch · First clients onboarding now" reads better than "0+") or remove the stats section until you have 5+ real clients
  • Check why "About" and "Let's Talk" sections show "Loading..." — same flash-of-placeholder problem as the app
  • Decide: should cold ad traffic land on himanshurajput.com (personal brand, services-heavy) or a dedicated stitchingmanagerpro.com page (product-only, no distraction)? See note below.
💡

Decide your landing page strategy now — it affects every ad and script below

You own three domains for a reason. For a cold prospect clicking a WhatsApp link or an ad, himanshurajput.com is the wrong destination — it's a personal portfolio with six other services competing for attention (AI chatbots, Zoho CRM, Odoo ERP). A tailoring shop owner doesn't care that you also build WhatsApp bots; it dilutes trust in the specific product. Recommended split:

  • stitchingmanagerpro.com → the only link in every ad, WhatsApp message, and YouTube Shorts bio. Product-only, one CTA: "Book Free Demo"
  • himanshurajput.com → used only when someone asks "who built this" or for your personal brand / other services, not as the primary funnel
  • swarooponline.com → company-level credibility page, used in footers/legal mentions, not as primary CTA destination

This plan assumes stitchingmanagerpro.com becomes your single landing page. If it's not built yet, that's the one task that unlocks everything else — see Phase 1 in the roadmap tab.

The Sequence

90-Day Roadmap: Prove It, Then Scale It

Three phases, each unlocking the next. Don't skip ahead to paid ads just because it feels more "real" — a pre-launch product with no testimonials and a ₹10k budget will waste most of that money learning lessons you can learn for free in Phase 1.

1

Prove It Sells

Days 1–30 · ₹0 ad spend

Direct WhatsApp outreach + local shop visits. Goal: 5-10 real paying customers using only free channels. This is your proof that the product converts before you pay anyone to look at it.

  • Fix site issues (see Fix First tab)
  • Outreach to 50+ shops directly
  • Get 5-10 paying customers
  • Collect 3+ testimonials/video reviews
  • Start posting YouTube Shorts (3-4/week)
2

Build Proof & Audience

Days 31–60 · ₹0–3,000 ad spend

Use real customer results as content. Start a tiny WhatsApp/Instagram boost budget only to amplify content that's already organically working — never to launch cold, untested ad copy.

  • Daily/near-daily YouTube Shorts using real customer clips
  • Boost only the best-performing organic post (₹50-100/day max)
  • Set up Meta Pixel + Google Tag now (free, takes 15 min, no reason to delay)
  • Build referral incentive: 1 free month per referral
  • Target: 15-25 total paying customers
3

Scale With Paid Ads

Days 61–90 · ₹5,000–10,000 ad spend

Now you have testimonials, a tested landing page, pixel data, and proof of what messaging converts. This is when Facebook/Instagram/Google ads stop being a gamble and start being a multiplier.

  • Launch 1 platform first (Meta, not Google — cheaper to test on)
  • Use winning organic hook as the ad creative
  • Retarget website visitors who didn't convert
  • Add Google Search ads only once Meta CPL is proven under ₹150
  • Target: 30-40 total paying customers, MRR ₹25,000-50,000+

Why This Order

Why WhatsApp/local comes before paid ads — not after

With zero customers, you have no testimonials, no case studies, no proof the product solves the problem at the price you're charging, and no data on which message resonates. Running paid ads right now means you're paying to discover all of that — at ₹100-250 per lead, your ₹10,000 budget buys you roughly 40-100 leads with no way to know if your ad copy, your landing page, your pricing, or your demo pitch is the weak link. Sell to 10-20 people directly first using free channels. You'll learn exactly which sentence makes a shop owner say yes, which objection comes up every single time, and what they actually call this problem in their own words — then your ad copy writes itself and converts 2-3x better than anything written from a desk.

Content Engine

4 Repeatable Short Formats — Mix These Weekly

1. Problem-Agitation Hook
Open on a relatable pain point ("Diary mein order dhundte dhundte customer chala gaya?"), then 10-15 seconds of screen-recorded app solving it. These perform best for cold reach — they don't require anyone to already know your product.
2. Feature-in-15-Seconds
Pick ONE feature per video (e.g. just the WhatsApp auto-alert, just the PDF export). Single-feature clips are easier to make consistently than trying to show everything every time.
3. Real Customer Reaction (Phase 2+, once you have customers)
Film a 15-second clip of an actual shop owner using the app or saying one sentence about it. This is your highest-converting format once available — nothing beats a real person who isn't you saying it works.
4. "Before vs After" Split
Messy diary photo vs clean Kanban board screen. Extremely fast to make, strong visual contrast, works well as a recurring weekly series.

💡

Posting tips that matter more than production quality

  • Post consistently (3-5/week) rather than occasionally going for a "perfect" video — the algorithm rewards frequency early on
  • Always caption in Hinglish/Hindi if your audience is Hindi-speaking shop owners — don't default to English captions
  • Put the link to stitchingmanagerpro.com in your bio and mention "link in bio" verbally/on-screen in every video
  • Reuse the same screen-recording demo footage across multiple Shorts with different hooks/voiceovers — you don't need new footage every time
  • Once you have 3+ real customers, customer-reaction clips will outperform anything you make yourself — prioritize asking for those
Phase 3 · Days 61-90 · ₹5,000-10,000

Paid Ads — Only After Phases 1 & 2

By the time you reach this tab for real, you should have 15-25 paying customers, at least 3 testimonials, a tested landing page, and a clear sense of which message converts. Don't open this tab on Day 1 and start spending — it'll waste money relative to where it'll go after Phase 1-2 proof.

🎯

Start with one platform, not three at once

With ₹5,000-10,000/month, splitting across Facebook + Instagram + Google dilutes your budget so much that no single campaign gets enough data to optimize. Start with Meta (Facebook + Instagram run from one campaign) since it's cheaper per click for awareness-stage products. Add Google Search only once you have a proven Meta cost-per-lead under ₹150 — Google clicks are typically 2-3x more expensive but higher-intent.

Meta

Facebook + Instagram — Lead Gen Campaign

Objective: Leads (use the on-platform lead form, not website clicks, for the first test — fewer steps means cheaper leads)

Audience: Location: your state + 2-3 neighboring states. Interests: small business owners, tailoring, fashion design. Age: 28-55. Start broad — let Meta's algorithm find the actual converters rather than over-narrowing on day one.

Creative: Use your best-performing organic Short as the ad video. Don't write new ad copy from scratch — use the exact hook that already got engagement organically.

Budget: ₹150-200/day to start, run for at least 4-5 days before judging results (Meta needs ~50 conversions per ad set to exit learning phase, but at this budget judge on cost-per-lead trend, not full optimization).
Google

Search Ads — Add Only After Meta Works

Why later: Search ads target people already looking for a solution ("tailoring shop software", "darzi management app") — high intent but low volume in most Tier 2/3 markets, and CPCs add up fast on a small budget.

Keywords to start with: "tailoring shop software", "boutique management app India", "darzi app", "tailor order management software" — exact and phrase match only, avoid broad match until you have negative keywords built up.

Budget: ₹100-150/day, capped tightly. Check search terms report daily for the first week and add negative keywords aggressively (you'll get irrelevant clicks like "tailoring courses" or "sewing machine price").
Setup Now, Use Later

Two Things to Set Up Today, Even Though You Won't Run Ads Yet

Meta Pixel on stitchingmanagerpro.com — free, 15 minutes, and every visitor between now and Phase 3 builds a retargeting/lookalike audience you'll use later at no extra cost
Google Tag on the same site, same reason — conversion data compounds, and there's no downside to having it running from day one
Retargeting

Retargeting — Your Highest-ROI Ad Budget Line

Once the Pixel has been live a few weeks, run a small (₹50-100/day) retargeting campaign to anyone who visited stitchingmanagerpro.com but didn't book a demo. These people already know what the product is — they just need a nudge, often with a testimonial or a limited-time offer ("First month ₹349 — this week only"). Retargeting consistently produces the lowest cost-per-lead of any campaign type, so even on a tiny budget, don't skip it once you have site traffic to retarget.
